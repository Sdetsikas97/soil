    About this atlas

    The atlas maps soil spectral libraries (SSLs) identified in a systematic review of the literature. The unit of analysis is the library, not the paper: each library is counted once, however many papers describe or use it.

    Screening criteria

    C1 Soil-specific
    Spectra are of soils. General mineral, rock or vegetation libraries are excluded.
    C2 Spectroscopic
    The study measures or analyses soil spectra (vis-NIR, NIR, MIR or LWIR).
    C3 Library-scale data
    Spectra come from, or are added to, an identifiable library spanning multiple fields or sites, or the authors present the dataset as a library. Single-field, single-site or trial studies with no library are excluded.
    Decision
    Included when C1–C3 are all met. Duplicates are removed first; uncertain records are marked “Check”.
    Paper role
    Introduces the library · Extends / updates it · Methods for it (standardisation, harmonisation, QA) · Uses it.

    Map conventions

    Countries are shaded by the largest scale of library covering them: national, sub-national only, or multi-country only. Library points sit at the approximate centre of the study region where known, otherwise at the country centroid (or the mean of member-country centroids for multi-country libraries). Point locations are indicative only.
